# HLSMonitor Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 28, 2026

HLSMonitor is a diagnostic tool for HLS video streams, published by Kindly Ops, LLC, for iPhone,
iPad and Android. It is built so that your data stays on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. HLSMonitor has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, and no
servers of its own. We never see your stream URLs, your monitoring results, or anything else about
how you use the app.

## What stays on your device

- **Stream URLs you enter** are used to load the stream and, if "Remember URL" is on, are stored
  only in the app's local storage. On Android, that setting may be included in your own Android
  backup to your Google account.
- **Monitoring sessions** (segment counts, download times, quality incidents) are recorded on the
  device and kept for 7 days so you can create quality reports. They are never transmitted and are
  not included in backups.
- **Audio loudness metering** computes loudness levels (LUFS and dB numbers) in real time, from
  the stream as it plays or, on iPhone and iPad, from the optional "HLSMonitor Loudness"
  screen-broadcast extension. No audio or video is recorded, stored, or uploaded.

## What leaves your device

- **Network requests to the streams and pages you choose to open.** The app's built-in player and
  browser load the addresses you enter or share to it, exactly as a web browser or video player
  would. Those requests go to those services' servers and are subject to their own privacy
  policies.
- **PDF quality reports you share.** Reports are created on the device and go only where you send
  them through the system share sheet.

## Permissions (Android)

HLSMonitor asks for no runtime permissions. It uses the Internet permission to load streams and
pages.

## Children

HLSMonitor is a professional tool with unrestricted web access and is not directed at children.
